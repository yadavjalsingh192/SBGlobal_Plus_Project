## 1. FF-01 — Healthcare & Diagnostics: four MS to Foundation depth `[AC — anchors SD]`

### 1.1 HLT-HMS — Hospital Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** hospital-wide operations — OPD/IPD patient flow, beds/wards, clinical orders, operation theatre, nursing, discharge, medical records; billing binds to Core Billing (F-01 §5). **Actors:** Hospital Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Ward Manager, OT Coordinator, Billing Executive, MRD Officer, Patient.
**Modules:** OPD (appointments, queue/tokens, consultation, e-prescription) · IPD (admission, bed/ward allocation, transfers, nursing charts, vitals, medication administration record, discharge summary) · Emergency (triage levels) · OT (scheduling, pre-op checklist, surgery notes, recovery) · Clinical Orders (lab/radiology/pharmacy routed to HLT-LIS/RIS/PMS) · Wards & Beds · Hospital Billing binding · MRD (records, retention).
**Workflows (states):**
- **OPD:** Appointment/Walk-in → Check-in → Vitals/Triage → Consultation → Orders/e-Prescription → Billing → Follow-up/Closed.
- **IPD:** Admission Request → Bed Allocated → In-Care (rounds, nursing charts, orders) → Discharge Initiated → Clearances (pharmacy/billing/nursing) → Discharge Summary Approved → Discharged → MRD Archive; branches: Transfer (ward/bed, audited), LAMA/Referred-out (reason recorded).
- **OT:** Request → Scheduled → Pre-op Checklist Complete → In-Surgery → Recovery → Notes Approved → Closed; Cancelled (reason).
**Business rules:** **BR-HLT-05 Admission gate:** admission activates only with bed allocation + recorded consent. **BR-HLT-06 Discharge gate:** discharge completes only when dues are settled or approved credit/corporate transfer exists AND the treating doctor has approved the discharge summary. **BR-HLT-07 Medication verification:** high-risk drug administration requires second-staff verification; both identities logged. **BR-HLT-08 Transfer audit:** every bed/ward transfer records reason, authorizer, timestamps — immutable.
**Masters:** wards, bed types/beds, specialties, procedure/tariff catalog, consent templates, discharge-summary templates, triage levels. **KPIs:** bed occupancy %, average length of stay, OPD throughput, OT utilization, discharge TAT, readmission rate, revenue per bed. **Events:** patient.admitted/transferred/discharged, ot.scheduled/completed, order.placed, summary.approved. **Dependencies:** HMS↔LIS/RIS/PMS (orders/results/dispensing), HMS↔Core Billing, HMS↔suite Inventory. **Acceptance:** OPD and IPD lifecycles complete with full audit; BR-HLT-05…08 pass/fail scenarios; tenant + industry-context isolation.

### 1.2 HLT-RIS — Radiology Information System
**Purpose/Scope:** imaging order-to-report lifecycle; PACS future-ready (F-07 §1.4). **Actors:** Radiologist, Radiographer/Technician, Receptionist, Referring Doctor, Patient.
**Modules:** imaging orders · modality scheduling (slots per modality) · contrast & consent management · modality worklist · exam capture · radiologist reading queue · structured report templates · peer/second read · critical-findings alerting · report distribution (QR-verified, per F-07 report standards) · TAT monitoring · repeat/dose log-ready.
**Workflow (states):** Order → Scheduled → Patient Prepared (consent/contrast screen) → Exam Performed → Images Available → Reading → Draft Report → Second Read (configurable by exam type) → Approved → Published → Archive; branches: Rejected/Repeat (reason + approval), Cancelled.
**Business rules:** **BR-HLT-09 Contrast gate:** contrast study blocked when a recorded allergy/contraindication exists unless a radiologist override with justification is logged. **BR-HLT-10 Critical finding:** critical imaging finding triggers immediate alert to referring doctor + delivery log (radiology counterpart of BR-HLT-01). **BR-HLT-11 Repeat stewardship:** repeat exam requires reason code + supervisor approval. **BR-HLT-12 Report immutability:** published report immutable; corrections only as versioned addenda.
**Masters:** modalities, exam/procedure catalog, prep instructions, report templates, reason codes. **KPIs:** TAT per modality, repeat rate, reading backlog, modality utilization, critical-alert acknowledgment time. **Events:** exam.ordered/performed, report.approved/published, finding.critical. **Dependencies:** RIS↔HMS/CMS (orders), RIS↔Core Billing, RIS↔Core document/trust services. **Acceptance:** order→published-report cycle with audit; BR-HLT-09…12 scenarios; isolation.

### 1.3 HLT-PMS — Pharmacy Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** pharmacy dispensing, drug-stock control, regulatory registers for hospital/clinic/retail pharmacy operations. **Actors:** Pharmacist, Pharmacy Manager, Store Keeper, Doctor (e-prescription), Patient.
**Modules:** e-prescription intake · counter/OTC sales · dispensing with batch/expiry selection (FEFO) · interaction/allergy check hooks (F-05 document-intelligence optional) · scheduled/controlled-drug register · ward indents (IPD) · returns & recalls · purchases/GRN & supplier management · inter-pharmacy transfers · stock adjustments.
**Workflows (states):** **Dispensing:** Prescription Received → Validated (interaction/allergy/stock) → Billed → Dispensed (batch recorded) → Closed; Partial-fill branch retains balance. **Ward supply:** Indent → Approved → Issued → Administration link (HMS MAR). **Recall:** Notice → Batch Trace → Quarantine → Return/Dispose → Report.
**Business rules:** **BR-HLT-13 Expiry guard:** expired batches cannot be dispensed; near-expiry alerts per configured horizon. **BR-HLT-14 Scheduled-drug gate:** scheduled/controlled drugs dispense only against a valid prescription reference with pharmacist identity recorded in the register. **BR-HLT-15 FEFO default:** first-expiry-first-out batch selection is default; overrides require reason and are audited. **BR-HLT-16 Recall traceability:** a recalled batch traces every dispensed unit to patient/sale within the tenant.
**Masters:** drug catalog (generic/brand, form, strength), drug schedules/classes, suppliers, tax classes, storage conditions. **KPIs:** stock turnover, expiry write-off %, fill TAT, stock-out rate, scheduled-register compliance. **Events:** prescription.dispensed, batch.recalled, stock.low, indent.issued. **Dependencies:** PMS↔HMS/CMS (prescriptions/indents), PMS↔Core Billing, PMS↔suite inventory masters. **Acceptance:** dispensing and recall cycles with audit; BR-HLT-13…16 scenarios; isolation.

### 1.4 HLT-CMS — Clinic Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** outpatient clinic/polyclinic operations — appointment-to-follow-up encounter lifecycle for single and multi-doctor practices. **Actors:** Clinic Admin, Doctor, Clinical Assistant, Receptionist, Patient.
**Modules:** appointments/queue/tokens · encounter records (vitals, clinical notes, ICD-mapped diagnosis, e-prescription) · in-clinic procedures · follow-up scheduling · referrals out (HMS/LIS/RIS with consented summary) · clinic billing binding · patient communication (reminders, reports) · tele-consultation (entitlement-gated).
**Workflow (states):** Appointment/Walk-in → Token → Vitals → Consultation → (Orders / Procedure / Prescription) → Billing → Follow-up Booked → Closed; branches: No-show (policy), Referred (summary attached).
**Business rules:** **BR-HLT-17 Encounter lock:** encounters immutable after doctor sign-off; corrections as addenda. **BR-HLT-18 Referral consent:** outbound referral carries only the consented clinical summary. **BR-HLT-19 No-show policy:** configurable no-show fee/rebooking rule, audited. **BR-HLT-20 Tele-consult gate:** tele-consultation available only when the entitlement chain grants it (F-01 §5).
**Masters:** visit types, procedure catalog, diagnosis favorites, fee schedules, follow-up protocols. **KPIs:** consultations/day, average wait time, follow-up adherence, revenue per doctor, no-show rate. **Events:** encounter.signed, referral.sent, followup.booked. **Dependencies:** CMS↔LIS/RIS/PMS (orders), CMS↔Core Billing/Communication. **Acceptance:** appointment→encounter→billing→follow-up cycle with audit; BR-HLT-17…20 scenarios; isolation.

## 2. FF-02 — thin MS to sibling depth + NGO-DMS review `[AC]`

### 2.1 EDU-CTM — Coaching & Training Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** enquiry-to-completion lifecycle for coaching centers, training and skill institutes (distinct from K-12/college administration owned by EDU-SMS/CUM). **Actors:** Center Admin, Counselor, Trainer, Student, Parent.
**Modules:** enquiry funnel (capture, follow-up ladder, conversion) · demo-class scheduling · batch planning (courses, schedules, capacity) · enrollment · fee plans/installments · batch attendance · study-material distribution (EDU-LMS binding) · test/mock series (EDU-EMS binding) · performance tracking · completion certificates.
**Workflows (states):** **Enrollment:** Enquiry → Counseling/Demo → Offer → Fee Plan Agreed → Enrolled (batch assigned) → Attending → Completed → Certificate Issued; Dropped (reason, refund per policy). **Batch:** Planned → Open (fills to capacity) → Running → Completed/Merged (audited).
**Business rules:** **BR-EDU-05 Batch capacity:** enrollment blocked at capacity; waitlist offered (parallel of BR-EDU-01 at batch granularity). **BR-EDU-06 Conversion integrity:** enquiry-to-enrollment conversion requires source + counselor recorded (marketing analytics depend on it). **BR-EDU-07 Installment default:** overdue installment → reminder ladder → access hold per policy — never data deletion (extends BR-EDU-03). **BR-EDU-08 Certificate gate:** completion certificate issues only when configured attendance % and assessment criteria are met.
**Masters:** courses, batches, fee plans, trainers, classrooms/slots, enquiry sources, certificate templates. **KPIs:** enquiry conversion %, batch fill %, fee realization, completion rate, trainer utilization. **Events:** enquiry.captured, student.enrolled, batch.completed, certificate.issued. **Dependencies:** CTM↔LMS/EMS, CTM↔Core Billing/Communication. **Acceptance:** enquiry→enrollment→attendance→completion cycle with audit; BR-EDU-05…08 scenarios; isolation.

### 2.2 RTL-RSM — Retail Store Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** the store-operations backbone: day lifecycle, staff shifts, cash control, in-store execution (POS transaction engine remains RTL-POS). **Actors:** Store Manager, Cashier, Floor Staff, Retail Admin.
**Modules:** store profile/hours/counters · staff shift roster · opening/closing checklists · cash management (floats, pickups, banking, variance) · price-change execution (from approved price lists) · local promotion execution · stock-take coordination (RTL-IWM) · service desk (returns/exchanges via RTL-OMS) · store dashboards.
**Workflows (states):** **Store day:** Opening Checklist → Float Issued → Trading (POS sessions) → Cash Pickups → Closing Checklist → Reconciliation → Day Closed; Variance → Review → Approved/Investigated. **Stock-take:** Scheduled → Counted → Variance Review → Adjustment Approved → Posted (IWM).
**Business rules:** **BR-RTL-05 Day-close gate:** day closes only when all POS sessions are reconciled; variance above threshold requires Store Manager approval. **BR-RTL-06 Price integrity:** shelf/system price changes apply only from an approved, effective price-list version (extends BR-RTL-03). **BR-RTL-07 Cash audit:** every float, pickup and variance entry is immutable with actor identity. **BR-RTL-08 Adjustment approval:** stock adjustments post only with approval + reason code.
**Masters:** counters, shift templates, checklist templates, reason codes, float denominations, promotion calendars. **KPIs:** sales per store, cash variance, shrinkage %, checklist compliance, POS session reconciliation rate. **Events:** store.day.opened/closed, cash.variance.flagged, stocktake.posted. **Dependencies:** RSM↔POS (sessions), RSM↔IWM (counts/adjustments), RSM↔OMS (returns). **Acceptance:** store-day and stock-take cycles with audit; BR-RTL-05…08 scenarios; isolation.

### 2.3 MFG-IWM — Inventory & Warehouse Management System (industrial configuration)
**Purpose/Scope:** industrial materials management serving production: receipts with QC gates, issues to orders, genealogy, counts (retail fulfillment logic remains RTL-IWM; shared Core-consuming pattern, separately configured — F-12 §2.5 pattern note upheld, no content copy). **Actors:** Store/Warehouse Keeper, Production Supervisor, QC Inspector, Procurement Officer.
**Modules:** warehouses/bins/storage types · GRN receipt (from MFG-PRO, QC gate per MFG-QMS) · putaway · reservations for released production orders · pick lists & issues to production · shop-floor returns · FG receipt from production · batch/lot/serial tracking (genealogy, BR-MFG-04) · cycle counts · scrap/disposal · inter-plant transfers · reorder monitoring (feeds BR-MFG-03).
**Workflows (states):** **Issue to production:** Order Released → Reservation → Pick List → Issued (batch captured) → WIP. **FG receipt:** Production Complete → QC → Putaway → Available. **Cycle count:** Planned → Counted → Variance Review → Approved → Adjusted (audited).
**Business rules:** **BR-MFG-05 Issue gate:** material issues only against released orders with reserved, QC-cleared stock. **BR-MFG-06 Hold enforcement:** QC-Hold stock cannot be issued, transferred, or dispatched (enforces BR-MFG-02 at the stock layer). **BR-MFG-07 Batch capture:** batch/lot/serial-managed materials record identity at every movement — genealogy has no gaps. **BR-MFG-08 No negative stock:** negative balances forbidden; corrections only via approved, reasoned adjustments.
**Masters:** storage types, bins, batch/serial profiles, UoM conversions, reason codes, count calendars. **KPIs:** inventory accuracy %, turns, stock-out incidents against released orders, dead stock value, count variance. **Events:** stock.issued/received/adjusted, lot.quarantined, reorder.triggered. **Dependencies:** IWM↔PMS (issues/FG), IWM↔PRO (GRN), IWM↔QMS (holds/dispositions). **Acceptance:** issue and FG-receipt cycles with unbroken genealogy; BR-MFG-05…08 scenarios; isolation.

### 2.4 PSV-SDM — Service Delivery Management System
**Purpose/Scope:** governed delivery of contracted/recurring services — SLAs, tickets/requests, deliverables with client acceptance (project structures remain PSV-PJM; time remains PSV-RTM). **Actors:** Delivery Manager, Consultant/Agent, Resource Manager, Client.
**Modules:** service contracts (scope, SLAs, entitled hours) · request/ticket intake & queues · prioritization + SLA clock binding (Core workflow engine) · deliverable tracking with acceptance · escalation matrix · client communication log · periodic service reviews · renewal coordination (PSV-CRM).
**Workflows (states):** **Ticket:** Received → Triaged (priority/SLA) → Assigned → In Progress → (Client Info Requested → Client Response) → Resolved → Client Acceptance → Closed; SLA Breach → Escalated (matrix). **Deliverable:** Planned → In Progress → Submitted → Client Review → Accepted / Revision (bounded) → Invoicing trigger (BR-PSV-04).
**Business rules:** **BR-PSV-05 Clock integrity:** SLA clocks pause only in defined client-wait states; pauses audited. **BR-PSV-06 Entitlement guard:** work beyond contracted hours requires approval or a change order before further billable logging. **BR-PSV-07 Acceptance precedes invoice:** deliverable/milestone invoicing requires recorded client acceptance (reinforces BR-PSV-04 at delivery level). **BR-PSV-08 Review cadence:** contracted service reviews are scheduled and tracked per contract terms.
**Masters:** service catalog, SLA matrices, priority schemes, escalation chains, contract templates, review templates. **KPIs:** SLA compliance %, first-response time, resolution time, backlog age, CSAT, contract profitability. **Events:** ticket.raised/resolved/breached, deliverable.accepted, contract.renewal.due. **Dependencies:** SDM↔CRM (contracts/renewals), SDM↔PJM (project-linked deliverables), SDM↔RTM (time), SDM↔Core Billing. **Acceptance:** ticket and deliverable cycles with audit; BR-PSV-05…08 scenarios; isolation.

### 2.5 NGO-DMS — Donor Management System (review closure)
CP-F1-003 blocker (2) required an NGO-DMS depth review. Review result: modules existed (F-12 §2.8) but the donor lifecycle and DMS-specific rules were implicit. Completed here:
**Modules (confirmed + completed):** donor registration & profiles · segments · engagement history · pledges · campaigns · communication preferences & consent · recognition tiers.
**Workflows (states):** **Donor lifecycle:** Prospect → First Donation (auto-created from NGO-DFM event) → Active → Lapsed (configurable inactivity window) → Reactivated. **Pledge:** Recorded → Reminder Schedule → Fulfilled / Partially Fulfilled / Cancelled.
**Business rules:** **BR-NGO-05 Consent-governed outreach:** communications honor per-channel consent; opt-outs enforced platform-wide. **BR-NGO-06 Pledge closure:** reminders stop automatically on fulfillment/cancellation. **BR-NGO-07 Confidential recognition:** donors flagged confidential (BR-NGO-03) are excluded from public recognition and donor-facing reports; segment recomputation on donation events respects the flag.
**KPIs:** donor retention %, lapsed-donor reactivation %, pledge fulfillment %, average gift, segment growth. **Dependencies:** DMS↔DFM (donations drive lifecycle), DMS↔Core Communication. **Review status: CLOSED — NGO-DMS is at sibling depth.**
